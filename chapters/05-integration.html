Integration with the existing project

Nothing already committed is discarded. PROVA‑MG absorbs it.

Part Five

How existing pieces fit

  • The license‑first pipeline becomes the data substrate: it produces the gold supervised set and the large licensed unlabeled set, plus the dialect, register, and license metadata that feed the latent‑variable model of 3.4.
  • The planned continued pre‑training of Wav2Vec2‑BERT produces one of the three teachers in 3.1.
  • The planned architecture comparison is repurposed: instead of choosing one winner, three teachers are kept and co‑infer; the comparison calibrates the routing function.
  • The planned pseudo‑labeling loop becomes the constrained EM of 3.3, with safeguards made explicit.
  • The planned dialect adaptation becomes 3.4, with dialect as a latent variable.
