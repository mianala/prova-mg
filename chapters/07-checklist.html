Validation checklist: every work item to prove it

A complete, ordered work plan. Each phase ends with a gate: a measurable result that must hold before the next phase is worth starting. Check items off as they are done; progress is saved automatically.

Part Seven

Phases, tasks, and gates

Validation progress 0 / 0 complete
P0

Foundations, infrastructure, and evaluation harness

You cannot prove anything without a trustworthy measuring stick. Build it first.

Gate 0   Baselines reproduce within a small margin of published WER, and the harness gives stable confidence intervals on a repeated run.
P1

Data substrate (license-first pipeline)

PROVA-MG consumes a gold supervised set and a large licensed unlabeled set. The pipeline produces both.

Gate 1   A licensed unlabeled pool large enough for pseudo-labeling exists, and the supervised set is large enough to train a routing function and a calibration head.
P2

Component 3.1 — Heterogeneous Foundation Model Co-Inference

Prove that routing across distinct teachers beats any single teacher, averaging, and voting.

Gate 2   Routing yields a statistically significant WER reduction over the best single teacher AND over averaging, on at least two test sets. If not, the disagreement signal is not informative and 3.1 must be revised.
P3

Component 3.5 — Confidence Calibration

Built early because 3.1 routing and 3.3 constraints both consume calibrated confidence.

Gate 3   Calibrated confidence has substantially lower ECE than raw confidence and improves routing decisions; the calibrator generalizes from supervised to unlabeled distribution.
P4

Component 3.2 — Typologically Informed Priors

Prove that an Austronesian family prior constrains decoding where a Malagasy-only LM cannot.

Gate 4   Either the typological prior gives a measurable, localized WER gain (component confirmed) or it does not (component becomes a documented negative result; 3.2 dropped from the load-bearing path).
P5

Component 3.3 — Constrained Variational EM

The theoretically heaviest component. Prove convergence, prove non-collapse, and prove the constraints matter.

Gate 5   The constrained loop improves WER monotonically (or near-monotonically) over iterations, the unconstrained loop demonstrably collapses, and each constraint has a measurable effect.
P6

Component 3.4 — Latent Dialect, Register, Code-Switching

Prove that joint inference of the latent variables beats treating them as separate subproblems.

Gate 6   Joint inference reduces WER on under-represented dialects and code-switched speech without degrading the majority dialect.
P7

Full-system validation and the thesis claim

Prove that the integrated system beats every subset and every published baseline.

Gate 7 (the thesis claim)   The integrated PROVA-MG system significantly outperforms every ablation and every baseline on independent, frozen, dialect-stratified test sets, and the gain is attributable to the integration rather than to any single component alone.
